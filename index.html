About me

I am an educational leader with over 34 years of teaching, 8 of them in school management. My record shows in student growth, in staff development and in how the schools I led performed. I lead through collaboration, adaptability and care for staff wellness. I have taught in South Africa and abroad, including five years in the United Kingdom, and bring both perspectives to my work.

My most recent leadership role was Head of Preparatory at Reddford House Northcliff. I kept teaching part time alongside it, which kept me close to new teaching methods and to what students need.
